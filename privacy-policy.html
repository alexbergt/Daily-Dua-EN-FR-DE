Privacy Policy

Daily Dua – EN FR DE

Last updated: April 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Daily Dua – EN FR DE app ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our application on Android devices.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Daily Dua app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your location data
  • Your device's phone number or IMEI
  • Financial or payment information
  • Photos, videos, or other media

2. Device Permissions

The app may request access to READ_PHONE_STATE. This permission is included by the underlying framework (Median.co) to ensure stable app functionality across different Android versions. We do not use this permission to read your phone number or device identifiers, and no data is collected or transmitted as a result.

3. Internet Access

The app loads its content from our hosted web page (GitHub Pages). This is a standard web request. No personal data is sent or stored during this process.

4. Local Storage

The app may use your device's local storage to save your favorite duas and language preferences. This data stays on your device only and is never transmitted to any server.

5. Third-Party Services

The app is built using Median.co, a web-to-app platform. Median.co may collect anonymised technical data for app stability purposes. Please refer to Median's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. The app contains Islamic supplications suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from any user.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
